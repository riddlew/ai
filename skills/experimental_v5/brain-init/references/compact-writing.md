# Compact Brain writing (v5)

Preserve all material meaning with the least useful prose. Keep exact contract fields, negative constraints, distinctive decisions, source identity/version/section, relevant actor/date and verified evidence. Prefer a stable reference to copying an entire requirement, plan or source. Use concise lists/tables when useful, not a fixed size limit.

Each consequential decision has one authoritative home; handoffs, progress and topic maps reference it. Preserve document IDs, `parents`, requirement refs, `depends_on`, and explicit amendment `amends`/`supersedes`. Historical v4 `status` and `parent_hashes` may remain as archival metadata but are not generated, validated for approval, or updated on editorial changes.

Do not remove evidence or combine distinct test outcomes merely to shorten a document. Do not silently convert proposals to established choices. For substantive changes, clarify consequential user choices and write a narrow amendment when useful; do not require an approval-only interaction. `brain-compact` remains opt-in, preview-first.
