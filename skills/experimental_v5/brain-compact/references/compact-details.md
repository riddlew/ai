# Manual compaction procedure (v5)

Only on explicit request. Identify the selected documents and their authoritative requirements, architecture, exact source references, decisions, amendments and execution evidence. Preserve each meaningful unique fact, qualifier, negation, requirement/example ID, actor/date where material, source/version and provenance.

1. Read target documents and necessary authoritative references; exclude private `.brain/.notes/`.
2. Propose a concise rewrite and preview unified diffs, highlighting removed redundancy and any ambiguous wording. Do not use an arbitrary token/character quota.
3. Apply **only when explicitly instructed to apply the preview**; do not insist on a separate approval ceremony or another skill solely to apply editorial changes.
4. Confirm that the diff preserves normative behavior, source citations, supersession, constraints and important chronology. Do not silently alter substantive requirements or merge independent verification events.
5. Respect write scopes, ownership and project version. No parent-hash refresh, descendant reapproval or automatic `brain-update`.

For major substantive edits rather than compaction, consult the user and use the relevant document owner or a focused amendment.
