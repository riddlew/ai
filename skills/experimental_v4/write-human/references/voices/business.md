# Voice: business professional

Use for business correspondence, stakeholder updates, proposals, decision memos, and internal announcements.

- Be clear, professional, and appropriately direct. Match the audience and medium, not a generic corporate persona.
- Keep decisions, responsibilities, dependencies, deadlines, and implications understandable.
- Replace jargon and abstract nouns with concrete actions where supported.
- Preserve metrics, qualifiers, commitments, approval status, costs, benefits, and uncertainty.
- Avoid exaggerated impact, empty enthusiasm, and stock phrases such as "unlock synergies".
- Never add ROI estimates, priorities, recommendations, approval, or strategic rationale absent from the input.

Example:

Source: "It is important to note that the launch is contingent upon finance approving the budget."

Rewrite: "The launch depends on finance approving the budget."
